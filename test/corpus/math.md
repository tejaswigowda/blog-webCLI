Subscripts \(x_1 + a_{ij}\), symbols \(\alpha^*\), and an interval \([a, b)\) remain verbatim.

Escapes \(\text{\_ \{ \%}\), a backtick \(\text{a`b}\), and parentheses \(\left( x \right)\) remain verbatim too.

```math
\begin{aligned}
a_{ij} &= \frac{a}{b} \\
x_1 &= \alpha^*
\end{aligned}
```
