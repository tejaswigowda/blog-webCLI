## Swim Platforms

We construct custom fitted swim platforms to be installed to the transom or the stern deck of a water vessel. Our platforms are made to order in the US and can be shipped internationally.

## Order Process

### Step 1

Upon submission of our [**quote form**](../request-a-quote/), you will receive a response within 3-4 business days with more information to proceed with the order.

### Step 2

A deposit will be requested depending on the order contents and a template kit will be sent to the address provided in the quote request. See **Template FAQs** for information about the template kit.

### Step 3

Once you have followed the template kit instructions and returned the kit to Swim-Platform, your order will begin construction.

### Step 4

Once the order is completed and ready to be shipped, the remainder of the balance will be required for the order to ship.

### **Notice**

While our platforms are custom-built and fit to each template that is received, Swim-Platform tries to maintain a **3-4 week turn-around** from receipt of the template to your order being shipped. Each order comes complete with mounting hardware and support hardware, but final fitting and mounting may require additional modification. Each platform requires installation by professionals (please see our **Installation FAQs** for further information). **ALL sales are final**.
