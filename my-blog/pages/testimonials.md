It truly has re-defined how we use the boat, since it provides so much space to either just hang out or have fun with. Our boy uses it as a diving platform. Everyone is impressed with the quality and it blends in with the boat as if it was factory. The ladder and ladder pocket are in the perfect location for maximum space usage and easy entry.
