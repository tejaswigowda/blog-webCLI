Your WordPress site, without the server.

If your site was hacked, or you pay for managed hosting so that it won't be, there is another way: plain files and static pages. Nothing to patch. Nothing to compromise.

Move my WordPress site

How it goes

Export your site from WordPress (Tools, Export, All content). Drop the file into the importer. Every post, page, draft and picture becomes a file in a repository you own, and your site is published as static pages. About ninety seconds of your attention. The whole walk-through.

What you get

8 KBfor a page, with no JavaScript file
0 msblocking time, on a throttled phone
0servers, databases or login pages to attack

Measured on a throttled cold connection (Slow 4G, 4x CPU), the median of five runs, with the method published: how, and what it does not say. A photograph-heavy post is as slow as its pictures; resize them first.

It can look like your site

You do not choose a WordPress theme, and you do not need one. The importer can measure your old site (its colours, its text width, its list layout) and match them. The typeface will not match exactly: a webfont becomes the nearest system font. Nothing of its stylesheet is copied.

Yours, as files

Your posts are markdown in a git repository with a full history, on your GitHub account. There is no account here to cancel and nothing to export again.
